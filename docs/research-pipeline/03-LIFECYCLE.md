### The three orderings that cost the most

**Stage 2 after stage 1.** Benchmark diligence ran after the method was
designed against an assumed schema. Two benchmarks were then rejected, and the
whole v1 framing died with them. Reading two label schemas is a one-hour task
that had to be paid for in weeks.

**Stage 12 after stage 11.** Leakage was found *after* the honest-verdict passes.
Every threshold, verdict and hedge in stage 11 had been computed on
contaminated numbers, so stage 13 is stage 11 done again — and the part that
nearly escaped was the **prose**, which the macro bridge does not update
(`d3ea4d4`).

**Stage 9 after stage 7 and 10.** Pipeline guards arrived after runs had already
been wasted. `e0fc924` names the category outright: *"run-wasting bugs"*. The
guards themselves are twenty lines.

### The shape stage 7 exposes

Eight paper versions in one day, with messages of the form *"fix 5 v4.5
errors"*, *"fix 6 visible v4.6 misalignments"*, *"harsh-review fixes"*. Each
version fixed errors the previous one introduced.

> When you are editing the rendered artifact faster than you can check it, the
> next commit should be a check, not another version.

## The order to actually use

```
Phase 0  Feasibility     ── schemas, leakage table, MDE, page limit, own prior work
Phase 1  Loud pipeline   ── assertions, guards, smoke run; prove it fails
Phase 2  Experiments     ── results land as generated files, artifacts committed
Phase 3  Interpretation  ── the claims table; both metrics; confounds named
Phase 4  Writing         ── prose from the claims table; abstract last
Phase 5  Conformance     ── template applied whole-document
Phase 6  Fit             ── cut to limit, prove no result lost
Phase 7  Deliverable     ── self-contained package, generated by a script
Phase 8  Sibling sweep   ── repeat 3–7 on every other artifact
```

Exit conditions are in [00-MASTER-PROMPT.md](00-MASTER-PROMPT.md) §The
eight-phase procedure.

### Why this order

- **0 before everything.** Schema, leakage and power are *inputs*. Discovered
  late they invalidate finished work — and in stage 12's case, work that had
  already been reviewed and hedged.
- **1 before 2.** A pipeline that cannot fail turns every later phase into manual
  inspection. This project hardened its pipeline at stage 9, after two rounds of
  compute.
- **2 before 3.** Never interpret while the numbers are still moving. A null
  interpreted on n=100 and a null interpreted with its MDE attached are
  different papers.
- **3 before 4.** This is the phase that did not exist in the original ordering.
  Interpretation happened *inside* writing, which is why the metric that reversed
  the conclusion was found by a reviewer rather than an author (C3).
- **4 before 5.** Formatting is a function of content. Reformatting after every
  result change was the largest source of rework.
- **6 after 5.** Conformance changes length. Cutting before conforming means
  cutting twice — and this project cut four times (G4).
- **8 always.** Two artifacts, one gets the attention. Divergence is invisible
  because each looks fine alone.

## What the project did right, and should be copied

Not everything went wrong. Five practices are the reason Paper 1's framing held
where the predecessor's shifted three times.

**1. A written scoping decision, with a kill rule.**
`docs/ASYU_SEED_SCOPE.md` locks what is in the conference paper and banks the
rest for the journal — and states an open empirical question with a decision
rule attached *in advance*:

> *"softmax wins on all 3 ⇒ the headline is purely orthogonality. σ wins on
> CORD/WildReceipt, softmax on SROIE ⇒ the regime distinction is real. mixed ⇒
> report what holds, frame conservatively.
> **Do NOT commit to a headline claim before MB + MF are run.**"*

The decision rule was written before the data. That is the whole discipline in
one paragraph.

**2. Pre-committed effect thresholds.** `5a1edfa` set ≥0.02 AUROC as the bar
*before* the verdict, and `8a0fded` then recorded the honest negative.

**3. A measurement artifact committed beside every derived number.** `006db2d`.

**4. The caveats deck.** A standalone document that states every limitation with
its real numbers, from first principles. See
[02-EVIDENCE-AND-CLAIMS.md §D](02-EVIDENCE-AND-CLAIMS.md).

**5. Build evidence in the commit message.** Every editorial-revision commit ends
with the same line: *"6 pages, 0 Overfull, 0 undefined refs/cites, 0 em-dashes,
guard PASS."* The claim and its evidence travel together.

**And the practice the successor plan added**, having learned from all of the
above — `non-key-handoff/non_key_channel_research_plan.md §0.4`:

> *"Power analysis (pre-committed; lesson from arith-gating). At pooled N≈1800
> the MDE at 80% power is ≈ Δ-AUROC 0.03. At per-corpus N=200 the MDE is
> ≈ 0.075. The kill thresholds are set above the pooled MDE. Any per-corpus cell
> below its corpus-MDE is explicitly flagged `[underpowered]` — never reported
> as a positive headline."*

That paragraph is Phase 0 done correctly. It exists because A6 and C1 happened.
