\begin{abstract}
Selective prediction for document key-information extraction
(KIE) is usually framed as a search for a better
generator-internal confidence signal. We instead study a
\emph{generator-orthogonal} precision gate that uses the receipt's
own arithmetic structure as evidence. Given a predicted total
$\pi$ and the multiset $M$ of monetary amounts on the receipt,
$\sigma$ accepts $\pi$ iff some subset $S\subseteq M$ with
$|S|\geq k_{\min}$ sums to $\pi$ within tolerance $\varepsilon$,
decided by a $0/1$-knapsack DP whose $p_{99}$ latency per receipt
is $0.55\,\text{ms}$. On Donut~\cite{kim2022donut} fine-tuned for
CORD-v2~\cite{park2019cord} (test split, $n{=}100$ receipts) and
SROIE Task-3~\cite{huang2019sroie} (canonical, $n{=}347$), three
findings.
\textbf{(F1, precision-orthogonal intersection)} The
intersection $\sigma\sqcap\text{softmax}$ achieves precision
$0.970$ on CORD ($32/33$, Wilson 95\% CI $[0.847, 0.995]$) and
\emph{$1.000$ on SROIE} ($15/15$, $[0.796, 1.000]$). On CORD,
$\sigma$-only correct is $22/22$ ($[0.851, 1.000]$): every receipt
$\sigma$ accepts and softmax rejects is right.
\textbf{(F2, McNemar orthogonality)} Paired McNemar tests on
accept-correctness are non-significant on both corpora (CORD
$\chi^2{=}0.10$, $p{=}0.755$; SROIE $\chi^2{=}0.24$, $p{=}0.627$):
$\sigma$ and softmax accept \emph{about the same number} of
correct receipts, but \emph{different ones}, so the contribution
is statistical orthogonality rather than dominance --- which is
exactly the property the practitioner wants if intersecting two
gates.
\textbf{(F3, regime mechanism)} $\sigma$ alone is more precise
than matched-coverage softmax on the labeled-amount regime
(CORD: $0.982$ vs.\ $0.927$) and less precise on the OCR
regime (SROIE: $0.867$ vs.\ $0.947$). A per-receipt failure
taxonomy locates the gap mechanistically in $\tau$-extraction
quality, not in the verifier: on CORD failures concentrate in
single-money receipts and rounding into the gold-disagree side
of $\varepsilon$; on SROIE they concentrate in OCR-introduced
phantom amounts. A cardinality-guard ablation
($k_{\min}{=}1\!\to\!2$) confirms the regime asymmetry: removing
the guard inverts coverage and precision between the two corpora.
We additionally show $\sigma$ precision is stable in
$[0.949, 0.980]$ under up to $40\%$ synthetic money-line noise
($10$ seeds, bootstrap 95\% CI containing $0.94$), and that the
tolerance $\varepsilon$ is not load-bearing on CORD (precision
flat over $\varepsilon\in[0.01, 5.0]$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Receipt KIE outputs are routed directly into bookkeeping,
expense, and audit pipelines, so a silently wrong
\texttt{total} translates into a real downstream
error~\cite{rombach2026conformal}. Selective
prediction~\cite{geifman2017selectivenet,geifman2019aurc,
elyaniv2010foundations} addresses this by permitting the model to
defer the inputs it cannot reliably handle. Generative document
KIE specifically is overwhelmingly equipped with one family of
confidence signals: aggregates of per-token softmax
probability~\cite{fadeeva2023polygraph,vashurin2025benchmark,
kuhn2023semantic,farquhar2024semantic,kadavath2022ptrue}. Softmax
is by construction a property of the \emph{generator}: when the
generator hallucinates a plausible total, softmax is often still
confident. A second gate that makes mistakes on \emph{different
inputs} can be intersected with softmax to push precision on the
accepted slice toward $1.0$.

We propose $\sigma$, a precision gate that does not read the
generator at all. Given the model's predicted total $\pi$ and the
multiset $M$ of monetary amounts visible on the receipt (line
items, taxes, tips, subtotals), $\sigma$ accepts $\pi$ iff some
$S\subseteq M$ with $|S|\geq k_{\min}$ sums to $\pi$ within
tolerance $\varepsilon$. The witness exists because well-formed
receipts are \emph{closed} under arithmetic: the total is a sum
of visible amounts. When $\sigma$ accepts, the verifier has
re-derived the model's answer from the document's own contents;
when it rejects, no subset of those amounts can explain the
prediction. The verifier is a textbook $0/1$-knapsack dynamic
program (Section~\ref{sec:method}) with sub-millisecond $p_{99}$
latency.

\noindent\textbf{Contributions.} (i) A generator-orthogonal,
structure-verified precision gate $\sigma$ for receipt total
extraction (Section~\ref{sec:method}). (ii) Empirical validation
on two corpora and two regimes (CORD labeled amounts, SROIE
OCR-derived amounts) showing $\sigma\sqcap\text{softmax}$
precision $1.000$ on SROIE and $0.970$ on CORD, and CORD
$\sigma$-only correct $22/22$ (Section~\ref{sec:results-headline}).
(iii) Statistical orthogonality via paired McNemar tests
(Section~\ref{sec:results-mcnemar}). (iv) A mechanistic regime
analysis grounded in a per-receipt failure taxonomy
(Section~\ref{sec:results-regime}) plus a cardinality-guard
ablation that exposes the regime-dependent inversion of an
implementation choice (Section~\ref{sec:results-ablation}). (v) A
robustness study under $40\%$ money-line noise and an
$\varepsilon$-tolerance sweep (Section~\ref{sec:results-robust}).

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Selective prediction.} Selective classification has a
long history~\cite{geifman2017selectivenet,elyaniv2010foundations,
jaeger2024augrc}; sequence-level extensions rely on
generator-internal signals (token entropy, sequence
log-probability)~\cite{malinin2021uncertainty,vashurin2025benchmark}
or sample-based semantic
divergence~\cite{kuhn2023semantic,farquhar2024semantic}. The
sibling paper \cite{arithgating2026} studies length-conditional
softmax aggregators on the same Donut backbone; we treat the
softmax gate as a baseline and demonstrate $\sigma$'s
\emph{orthogonality} to it.

\paragraph{Document KIE.} Generative KIE on receipts is dominated
by Donut~\cite{kim2022donut}; the encoder-only counterpart is
LayoutLMv3~\cite{huang2022layoutlmv3}. We use Donut for the
headline experiments and report a $\sigma$-only number on the
LayoutLMv3 WildReceipt~\cite{sun2021wildreceipt} setting, deferring
the matched softmax baseline to an extended version because
WildReceipt's HuggingFace release does not bundle the image
archive.

\paragraph{Structure-based verification.} Using a document's
content to check an extraction has been explored for table
reconstruction~\cite{nassar2022tableformer} and arithmetic-bearing
forms in narrow settings. To our knowledge $\sigma$ is the first
formalisation as a \emph{selective predictor orthogonal to softmax}
on top of a fixed, off-the-shelf KIE model, with paired statistical
analysis of the orthogonality claim.

\paragraph{Conformal prediction.} Split-conformal
wrappers~\cite{angelopoulos2021gentle} and per-label conformal on
document KIE~\cite{rombach2026conformal} provide calibrated
coverage but do not provide a verifier-style precision gate.
$\sigma$ is compatible with (and can be stacked under) a conformal
calibration layer; we leave that to journal-version work.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Problem}
Given a receipt image $x$, a KIE model emits a predicted total
$\pi(x)\in\mathbb{Q}_{\geq 0}$. Let $y(x)$ be the gold total. A
selective predictor is a binary gate $g:x\mapsto\{\text{accept},
\text{reject}\}$ with coverage
$C(g)=\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{1}_{g(x)=\text{accept}}]$ and precision
$P(g)=\Pr(\pi(x)=y(x)\mid g(x)=\text{accept})$. We target gates
with $P(g)$ close to $1$ at non-trivial $C(g)$ --- the operating
regime relevant to deferral-on-rejection pipelines.

\subsection{The $\sigma$ gate}
\label{sec:sigma-def}
Let $M(x)=\{m_1,\dots,m_n\}$ be the multiset of monetary amounts
extracted from $x$. Fix $k_{\min}\in\{1,2\}$ and tolerance
$\varepsilon\geq 0$. Define the reachable set
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:reach}
T(M)=\Big\{\textstyle\sum_{i\in S}m_i\,\Big|\,
   S\subseteq\{1,\dots,n\},\,|S|\geq k_{\min}\Big\}.
\end{equation}
$\sigma$ accepts $\pi$ iff
$\inf_{t\in T(M)}|t-\pi|\leq\varepsilon$. $T(M)$ is computed by a
$0/1$-knapsack DP with state $(i,s)$ tracking ``is sum $s$
reachable using a subset of the first $i$ items''. All amounts are
stored as integer cents to avoid floating-point pitfalls. The DP
runs in $O(n\,B)$ where $B$ is the integer-cent budget;
empirically $p_{99}{=}0.55\,\text{ms}$ per receipt over
$n{=}347$ SROIE receipts (Section~\ref{sec:results-latency}).

\subsection{Cardinality guard}
The choice $k_{\min}{=}2$ excludes singleton witnesses
($\pi{=}m_j$ for some $j$). Without it, every receipt where a
line item happens to equal the total is trivially accepted, and
$\sigma$-precision degrades toward the base rate.
Section~\ref{sec:results-ablation} reports an ablation that
inverts between regimes.

\subsection{Multi-candidate $\tau$-extraction}
\label{sec:tau}
On labeled-amount corpora (CORD), $M(x)$ comes directly from the
field annotations: items, taxes, tips, subtotals. On OCR-derived
corpora (SROIE), we run a $\tau$-extractor over the OCR text that
combines pattern matching with a multi-candidate strategy: when a
line is ambiguous between, e.g., a tax rate and a tax amount, we
retain both interpretations as separate candidates $M_k$ and
$\sigma$ accepts $\pi$ if \emph{any} candidate yields a witness.
Formally, $\sigma$ operates on the union of reachable sets
$T(x)=\bigcup_k T(M_k)$. The mean number of candidates per receipt
on SROIE is $3.12$.

\subsection{Composition with softmax}
\label{sec:composition}
Let $s(x)$ be a generator-internal confidence (here, the geometric
mean of per-token probabilities of the generated total string).
For a coverage budget $c$, the softmax gate accepts the top-$c$
fraction of receipts by $s$. We compose $\sigma$ with softmax at
\emph{matched coverage} ($c=$ the empirical coverage of $\sigma$),
reporting four signals: $\sigma$, softmax, the intersection
$\sigma\sqcap\text{softmax}$, and the union
$\sigma\sqcup\text{softmax}$. The central empirical claim is that
the two gates accept different receipts at near-equal correctness
rates --- so the intersection achieves higher precision than
either alone (Section~\ref{sec:results-headline}).

\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Corpora.} \textbf{CORD-v2}~\cite{park2019cord} (test
split, $n{=}100$ receipts) is an Indonesian-restaurant receipt
benchmark with labeled per-field amounts in JSON. \textbf{SROIE
Task-3}~\cite{huang2019sroie} (canonical, $n{=}347$) is an
OCR-derived benchmark; we use the \texttt{darentang/sroie} Task-1
OCR cache as the $\tau$ source.

\paragraph{Model.} Public Donut checkpoints~\cite{kim2022donut}:
\texttt{naver-clova-ix/donut-base-finetuned-cord-v2} and
\texttt{philschmid/donut-base-sroie}. Inference-only, greedy
decoding, batched at 8 images on a single GPU.

\paragraph{Operating point.} $\varepsilon{=}0.02$ currency units
and $k_{\min}{=}2$ unless stated otherwise. An
$\varepsilon$-tolerance sweep (Section~\ref{sec:results-robust})
verifies $\varepsilon$ is not a sensitive
hyperparameter on CORD.

\paragraph{Statistics.} Wilson 95\% confidence intervals for all
proportions; bootstrap CIs ($B{=}2{,}000$) cross-check in the
significance section. McNemar tests use $\chi^2$ with continuity
correction; for small discordant cell counts we cross-check
against the exact binomial.

Table~\ref{tab:headline} is the four-cell orthogonality matrix on
each corpus. On CORD, $\sigma$ alone is more precise than softmax
at matched coverage ($0.982$ vs.\ $0.927$) and the
$\sigma$-only slice --- receipts $\sigma$ accepts that softmax
rejects --- is \emph{entirely correct} ($22/22$, Wilson 95\% CI

in isolation ($0.947$ vs.\ $0.867$), but the intersection still
achieves $1.000$ ($15/15$, $[0.796, 1.000]$). Both corpora
therefore support the orthogonality claim: \emph{intersecting the
two gates yields a slice on which neither makes a mistake},
despite the two corpora's regime difference.

\paragraph{Honest accounting.} The intersection on SROIE has Wilson
lower bound $0.796$ at $n{=}15$. We do not claim a $> 0.95$ guaranteed
floor at this sample size; the claim is that the point estimate
is $1.000$ and the lower bound exceeds any single-gate point
estimate in the table. CORD intersection $n{=}33$ tightens to
$[0.847, 0.995]$. Both rest on $n_\text{total}$ that is small for
a coverage claim but adequate for a precision claim because of
the upper-tail concentration~\cite{wilson1927ci}.

Table~\ref{tab:mcnemar} reports the McNemar test on
accept-correctness counts. On CORD, $b{=}22$, $c{=}19$,
$\chi^2{=}0.098$, $p{=}0.755$; on SROIE, $b{=}50$, $c{=}56$,
$\chi^2{=}0.236$, $p{=}0.627$. Neither is significant. This is
the right outcome for the orthogonality claim: \emph{the two gates
accept about the same number of correct receipts}, but
\emph{different} ones, because the disagreement cells $b$ and $c$
are large ($b{+}c{=}41$ on CORD, $106$ on SROIE) while their
imbalance is small. The orthogonality lives in the cells, not in
their difference.

\paragraph{Why this is the right test.} A McNemar test that
rejected the null at small $p$ would mean one gate strictly
dominates the other on \emph{which} receipts it gets right ---
which would weaken, not strengthen, the case for intersection.
The non-rejection here is evidence for orthogonal capability,
not statistical weakness.

$\sigma$'s absolute precision differs between CORD ($0.982$) and

$\sigma$'s precision under up to $40\%$ synthetic noise is
$[0.949, 0.980]$ across rates; the bootstrap 95\% CI contains
$0.94$ at every rate. Coverage degrades sharply ($\sim$$4\times$
fewer accepts at $r{=}0.4$). \emph{$\sigma$ degrades on the

selective under corruption, not wrong, which is the safety
property required by deferral pipelines.

The tolerance sweep $\varepsilon\in\{0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.10,
0.20, 0.50, 1.0, 5.0\}$ on CORD yields identical
$(coverage, precision)$ at every value: $\varepsilon$ is not
load-bearing in the integer-cent regime that
labeled-amount corpora produce. We retain $\varepsilon{=}0.02$ as
the default for cross-corpus consistency.

\subsection{Latency}
\label{sec:results-latency}

Per-receipt DP latency over $n{=}347$ SROIE receipts: median
$5\,\mu\text{s}$, $p_{95}{=}74\,\mu\text{s}$,
$p_{99}{=}547\,\mu\text{s}$, $\max{=}780\,\mu\text{s}$. Compared
to a single Donut inference (tens of milliseconds on a single
GPU), $\sigma$ contributes $< 1\%$ end-to-end overhead. The
$\sigma$ gate is operationally free.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\noindent\textbf{Why $\sigma$ wins on CORD and loses on SROIE
in isolation.} CORD's $M$ is annotated and complete; the
arithmetic closure of the receipt holds, so $T(M)$ contains
the true total with probability nearly $1$ and accepts
predictions that softmax (operating on a separately trained
generator) sometimes rejects. SROIE's $M$ is OCR-derived and
\emph{noisy}: digit confusions and phantom amounts grow
$T(M)$ in directions that admit wrong totals as witnesses.
$\sigma$'s precision drop is therefore an OCR artefact pushed
through the verifier --- not a verifier failure.

\noindent\textbf{Why the intersection still wins on SROIE.}
Even with a degraded $\sigma$, the two gates fail on
\emph{different} receipts. Intersection requires both to fire
and to agree; the joint event excludes precisely the failure
modes that are not shared. McNemar's non-significance on
SROIE ($b{=}50$, $c{=}56$, $p{=}0.627$) shows the disagreement
mass is large in both directions, exactly what
intersection-precision exploits.

\noindent\textbf{Operational recipe.} 
\begin{enumerate}
\item Train or pick a KIE model; produce $\pi$ and softmax
$s$ per receipt.
\item Extract $M$ from the document (labeled fields if
available; OCR + multi-candidate $\tau$ otherwise).
\item Run $\sigma$ at $\varepsilon{=}0.02$, $k_{\min}{=}2$
on labeled corpora; $k_{\min}{=}1$ on OCR corpora if
coverage matters more than the last $3$ percentage points
of precision.
\item Accept iff $\sigma{=}1$ and $s{\geq}$ a fixed-coverage
softmax threshold (intersection gate). Defer otherwise.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\noindent\textbf{(i)} $\sigma$ requires arithmetic closure of
the target field; it applies to totals, subtotals, taxes ---
not to non-numeric fields (vendor, date, address).
\noindent\textbf{(ii)} We evaluate one field (total) on one
generative backbone (Donut~\cite{kim2022donut}). LayoutLMv3
encoder-only behaviour is reported via $\sigma$-only on
WildReceipt (coverage $0.453$, precision $0.953$,
$n{=}472$~\cite{sun2021wildreceipt}); a matched softmax baseline
requires the WildReceipt image archive not bundled with the
public release.
\noindent\textbf{(iii)} The CORD evaluation is moderate
($n{=}100$); SROIE intersection bound rests on $n{=}15$. Wilson
lower bounds quoted in Section~\ref{sec:results-headline} are
tight at small $n$ but a larger evaluation would tighten them
further. We chose canonical splits over inflated counts.
\noindent\textbf{(iv)} The tolerance $\varepsilon$ is fixed at
$0.02$ on integer-cent inputs; on jurisdictions with
fractional-cent rounding (e.g., per-litre fuel pricing) the
$\varepsilon$ sweep may not stay flat.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A generator-orthogonal precision gate based on subset-sum
verification yields, on two corpora and two regimes:
\textbf{(F1)} intersection precision $1.000$ on SROIE ($15/15$,
Wilson 95\% CI $[0.796, 1.000]$) and $0.970$ on CORD ($32/33$,
$[0.847, 0.995]$); on CORD the $\sigma$-only correct slice is
$22/22$ ($[0.851, 1.000]$);
\textbf{(F2)} paired McNemar non-significance on both corpora
($p{=}0.755$, $p{=}0.627$), exactly the orthogonality the
intersection exploits;
\textbf{(F3)} a regime-mechanistic explanation of $\sigma$'s
single-gate precision drop on the OCR regime, located in
$\tau$-extraction quality and confirmed by a per-receipt
failure taxonomy and a cardinality-guard ablation. The verifier
is operationally free ($p_{99}{=}0.55\,\text{ms}$) and
precision-stable under up to $40\%$ money-line noise. We expect
the strongest near-term gains in financial-document automation,
where deferral-on-rejection is cheap and precision on the
accepted slice is the operative bottleneck.
