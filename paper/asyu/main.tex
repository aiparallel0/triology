\begin{abstract}
Selective prediction for document key-information extraction (KIE)
is usually framed as a search for a better generator-internal
confidence signal. We instead study a \emph{generator-orthogonal}
precision gate that uses the receipt's own arithmetic structure as
evidence. Given a predicted total $\pi$ and the multiset $M$ of
monetary amounts on the receipt, $\sigma$ accepts $\pi$ iff some
subset $S\subseteq M$ with $|S|\geq k_{\min}$ sums to $\pi$ within
tolerance $\varepsilon$, decided by a $0/1$-knapsack DP whose
per-receipt overhead is $0.0016\%$ of Donut generate latency on
the same GPU. We evaluate on a pooled three-corpus benchmark of
$n{=}919$ receipts: CORD-v2~\cite{park2019cord} test ($n{=}100$,
labeled amounts, Donut~\cite{kim2022donut}); SROIE
Task-3~\cite{huang2019sroie} canonical ($n{=}347$, OCR-derived
amounts, Donut); and WildReceipt~\cite{sun2021wildreceipt}
($n{=}472$, encoder-only token classification with
LayoutLMv3~\cite{huang2022layoutlmv3}). Three findings.
\textbf{(F1, precision-orthogonal intersection)} On the pooled
benchmark the intersection $\sigma\sqcap\text{softmax}$ achieves
Wilson 95\% precision lower bound $0.956$ at point estimate
$160/162$; $\sigma$ alone has lower bound $0.908$ at $323/344$,
softmax matched has lower bound $0.919$ at $326/344$; the
intersection exceeds both single-gate lower bounds. Per-corpus
intersection lower bounds ($0.847$ on CORD, $0.796$ on SROIE,
$0.952$ on WildReceipt) span all three regimes.
\textbf{(F2, McNemar orthogonality)} Paired McNemar on the
pooled discordant cells ($b{=}163, c{=}166$) gives
$\chi^2{=}0.012, p{=}0.912$. Per-corpus McNemar tests reach the
same conclusion (CORD $p{=}0.755$, SROIE $p{=}0.627$,
WildReceipt $p{=}0.941$ with perfectly symmetric $b{=}c{=}91$):
the two gates accept similar numbers of correct receipts on
\emph{different} subsets, exactly the property the intersection
exploits.
\textbf{(F3, regime mechanism)} $\sigma$ alone is more precise
than matched-coverage softmax on the labeled-amount regime
(CORD: $0.982$ vs.\ $0.927$); less precise on the OCR regime
(SROIE: $0.867$ vs.\ $0.947$); equally precise on the
encoder-only regime (WildReceipt: $0.953$ vs.\ $0.953$). A
per-receipt failure taxonomy locates the gap mechanistically in
$\tau$-extraction quality and on WildReceipt shows that even
\emph{equal} single-gate precision yields a strictly better
intersection.
We additionally show $\sigma$ precision is stable in
$[0.949, 0.980]$ under up to $40\%$ synthetic money-line noise,
the tolerance $\varepsilon$ is not load-bearing on CORD, and the
single CORD $\sigma$ miss localises to the $|T|\in[5,9]$
reachable-set bin.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Receipt KIE outputs are routed directly into bookkeeping,
expense, and audit pipelines, so a silently wrong
\texttt{total} translates into a real downstream
error~\cite{rombach2026conformal}. Selective
prediction~\cite{geifman2017selectivenet,geifman2019aurc,
elyaniv2010foundations} addresses this by permitting the model
to defer the inputs it cannot reliably handle. The standard
signal is softmax-derived (e.g.\ the geometric mean of per-token
probabilities of the predicted total
field)~\cite{malinin2021uncertainty,vashurin2025benchmark,
fadeeva2023polygraph}. Softmax is by construction a property of
the \emph{generator}: when the generator hallucinates a
plausible total, softmax is often still confident. A second
gate that makes mistakes on \emph{different inputs} can be
intersected with softmax to push precision on the accepted
slice toward $1.0$.

We propose $\sigma$, a precision gate that does not read the
generator at all. Given the model's predicted total $\pi$ and
the multiset $M$ of monetary amounts visible on the receipt
(line items, taxes, tips, subtotals), $\sigma$ accepts $\pi$
iff some $S\subseteq M$ with $|S|\geq k_{\min}$ sums to $\pi$
within tolerance $\varepsilon$. The witness exists because
well-formed receipts are \emph{closed} under arithmetic: the
total is a sum of visible amounts. The verifier is a textbook
$0/1$-knapsack dynamic program (Section~\ref{sec:method}) with
sub-millisecond $p_{99}$ latency.

\noindent\textbf{Contributions.} (i) A generator-orthogonal,
structure-verified precision gate $\sigma$ for receipt total
extraction (Section~\ref{sec:method}). (ii) Empirical
validation on a pooled three-corpus benchmark of $n{=}919$
receipts spanning two backbones (Donut generative,
LayoutLMv3 encoder-only) and three regimes (CORD labeled,
SROIE OCR, WildReceipt encoder-only) with Wilson 95\%
precision lower bounds for every cell
(Section~\ref{sec:results-headline}). (iii) Statistical
orthogonality via paired McNemar tests on the pooled and
per-corpus discordant cells, including a perfectly symmetric
WildReceipt result ($b{=}c{=}91$)
(Section~\ref{sec:results-mcnemar}). (iv) A mechanistic regime
analysis grounded in a per-receipt failure taxonomy
(Section~\ref{sec:results-regime}) plus a cardinality-guard
ablation that exposes the regime-dependent role of an
implementation choice
(Section~\ref{sec:results-ablation}). (v) A robustness study
under $40\%$ money-line noise and an $\varepsilon$-tolerance
sweep (Section~\ref{sec:results-robust}), and a
$\sigma$-reliability decomposition by reachable-set size
(Section~\ref{sec:results-reliability}).

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Selective prediction.} Selective classification has
a long history~\cite{geifman2017selectivenet,
elyaniv2010foundations,jaeger2024augrc}; sequence-level
extensions rely on generator-internal signals --- per-token
entropy, sequence
log-probability~\cite{malinin2021uncertainty,
vashurin2025benchmark,fadeeva2023polygraph} --- or
sample-based semantic divergence~\cite{kuhn2023semantic,
farquhar2024semantic}. We use the geometric mean of per-token
probabilities of the predicted total field as a single softmax
baseline. $\sigma$ is not an alternative softmax aggregator; it
is a different signal entirely.

\paragraph{Document KIE.} We evaluate on two architectures.
Donut~\cite{kim2022donut} is a generative
vision-encoder-decoder backbone that produces structured JSON;
its softmax confidence is the geometric mean of per-token
probabilities of the generated total string.
LayoutLMv3~\cite{huang2022layoutlmv3} is an encoder-only
token-classification backbone; its softmax confidence is the
geometric mean of per-token softmax probabilities at positions
classified as \texttt{Total\_value}. Both are the natural
sequence-likelihood signals for their respective architectures
and aggregate softmax over the predicted total-value tokens.

\paragraph{Structure-based verification.} Using a document's
content to check an extraction has been explored for table
reconstruction~\cite{nassar2022tableformer} and
arithmetic-bearing forms in narrow settings. To our knowledge
$\sigma$ is the first formalisation as a \emph{selective
predictor orthogonal to softmax} on top of fixed,
off-the-shelf KIE models, with paired statistical analysis of
the orthogonality claim across architectures.

\paragraph{Conformal prediction.} Split-conformal
wrappers~\cite{angelopoulos2021gentle} and per-label conformal
on document KIE~\cite{rombach2026conformal} provide calibrated
coverage but do not provide a verifier-style precision gate.
$\sigma$ is compatible with (and can be stacked under) a
conformal calibration layer; we leave that to journal-version
work.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{Problem}
Given a receipt image $x$, a KIE model emits a predicted total
$\pi(x)\in\mathbb{Q}_{\geq 0}$. Let $y(x)$ be the gold total.
A selective predictor is a binary gate
$g:x\mapsto\{\text{accept},\text{reject}\}$ with coverage
$C(g)=\mathbb{E}[\mathbb{1}_{g(x)=\text{accept}}]$ and
precision
$P(g)=\Pr(\pi(x)=y(x)\mid g(x)=\text{accept})$.

\subsection{The $\sigma$ gate}
\label{sec:sigma-def}
Let $M(x)=\{m_1,\dots,m_n\}$ be the multiset of monetary
amounts extracted from $x$. Fix $k_{\min}\in\{1,2\}$ and
tolerance $\varepsilon\geq 0$. Define the reachable set
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:reach}
T(M)=\Big\{\textstyle\sum_{i\in S}m_i\,\Big|\,
   S\subseteq\{1,\dots,n\},\,|S|\geq k_{\min}\Big\}.
\end{equation}
$\sigma$ accepts $\pi$ iff
$\inf_{t\in T(M)}|t-\pi|\leq\varepsilon$. $T(M)$ is computed
by a $0/1$-knapsack DP. All amounts are stored as integer
cents to avoid floating-point pitfalls. Empirically
$p_{99}{=}0.55\,\text{ms}$ per receipt
(Section~\ref{sec:results-latency}).

\subsection{Cardinality guard}
The choice $k_{\min}{=}2$ excludes singleton witnesses
($\pi{=}m_j$ for some $j$). Without it, every receipt where a
line item happens to equal the total is trivially accepted
and $\sigma$-precision degrades toward the base rate.
Section~\ref{sec:results-ablation} reports an ablation that
inverts between regimes.

\subsection{Multi-candidate $\tau$-extraction}
\label{sec:tau}
On labeled-amount corpora (CORD), $M(x)$ comes directly from
the field annotations. On OCR-derived corpora (SROIE), we run
a $\tau$-extractor over the OCR text that combines pattern
matching with a multi-candidate strategy: when a line is
ambiguous between, e.g., a tax rate and a tax amount, we
retain both interpretations as separate candidates $M_k$ and
$\sigma$ accepts $\pi$ if \emph{any} candidate yields a
witness. The mean number of candidates per receipt on SROIE
is $3.12$. On WildReceipt (encoder-only), $M(x)$ comes from
\texttt{Prod\_price\_value} annotations with per-row
aggregation; $\tau$ is the \texttt{Tax\_value} annotation.

\subsection{Composition with softmax}
\label{sec:composition}
Let $s(x)$ be the softmax confidence (architecture-specific:
see Section~\ref{sec:related}). For a coverage budget $c$, the
softmax gate accepts the top-$c$ fraction of receipts by $s$.
We compose $\sigma$ with softmax at \emph{per-corpus matched
coverage} ($c$ equal to the $\sigma$ coverage on that corpus),
reporting four signals: $\sigma$, softmax, the intersection
$\sigma\sqcap\text{softmax}$, and the union
$\sigma\sqcup\text{softmax}$. Per-corpus matching controls
for the regime-dependent operating point. The pooled table
aggregates the per-corpus matched accepts.

\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Pooled benchmark.}
\textbf{CORD-v2}~\cite{park2019cord} test split ($n{=}100$,
labeled per-field amounts in JSON);
\textbf{SROIE Task-3}~\cite{huang2019sroie} canonical
($n{=}347$, OCR-derived amounts; \texttt{darentang/sroie}
Task-1 OCR cache as $\tau$ source);
\textbf{WildReceipt}~\cite{sun2021wildreceipt} test
($n{=}472$, per-token annotations of price/tax/total values).
Pooled total $n{=}919$.

\paragraph{Models.}
Donut~\cite{kim2022donut} checkpoints
\texttt{naver-clova-ix/donut-base-finetuned-cord-v2} (CORD)
and \texttt{philschmid/donut-base-sroie} (SROIE).
LayoutLMv3~\cite{huang2022layoutlmv3} checkpoint
\texttt{Theivaprakasham/layoutlmv3-finetuned-wildreceipt}
(WildReceipt). Inference-only, greedy decoding for Donut.

\paragraph{Operating point.} $\varepsilon{=}0.02$ currency
units and $k_{\min}{=}2$ unless stated otherwise.

\paragraph{Statistics.} Wilson 95\% confidence intervals for
all proportions; bootstrap CIs ($B{=}2{,}000$) cross-check.
McNemar tests use $\chi^2$ with continuity correction.

Table~\ref{tab:mcnemar} reports the McNemar test on
accept-correctness counts. Pooled: $b{=}163, c{=}166,
\chi^2{=}0.012, p{=}0.912$. Per-corpus tests reach the same
conclusion. WildReceipt is particularly striking: $b{=}c{=}91$
exactly --- the two signals miss exactly the same number of
correct receipts on disjoint subsets, while their joint
accept set is $113/114 = 0.991$ correct.

\paragraph{Why this is the right test.} A McNemar test that
rejected the null at small $p$ would mean one gate strictly
dominates the other on \emph{which} receipts it gets right
--- which would weaken, not strengthen, the case for
intersection. The non-rejection here is evidence for
orthogonal capability, not statistical weakness.

\subsection{Latency}
\label{sec:results-latency}

Per-receipt DP latency over $n{=}347$ SROIE receipts: median
$5\,\mu\text{s}$, $p_{95}{=}74\,\mu\text{s}$,
$p_{99}{=}547\,\mu\text{s}$, $\max{=}791\,\mu\text{s}$.
Paired against Donut generate latency on the same GPU,
$\sigma$ contributes $0.0016\%$ of inference time --- the
gate is operationally free.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\noindent\textbf{Why $\sigma$ wins on CORD and loses on SROIE
in isolation.} CORD's $M$ is annotated and complete; SROIE's
$M$ is OCR-derived and \emph{noisy}: digit confusions and
phantom amounts grow $T(M)$ in directions that admit wrong
totals as witnesses. $\sigma$'s precision drop is an OCR
artefact, not a verifier failure.

\noindent\textbf{Why the intersection wins even when $\sigma$
and softmax are equally precise.} On WildReceipt the two
signals achieve identical single-gate precision ($0.953$) yet
the intersection rises to $0.991$ because they fail on
\emph{different} receipts (perfectly symmetric discordant
cells $b{=}c{=}91$, Figure~\ref{fig:venn} right panel). The
orthogonality argument does not require one signal to
dominate the other; it requires their errors to be disjoint.
The McNemar non-significance on all three corpora confirms
this.

\noindent\textbf{Cross-architecture survival.} Replacing
Donut (generative, autoregressive softmax over emitted total
tokens) with LayoutLMv3 (encoder-only, classification softmax
at predicted Total\_value positions) does not break the
orthogonality. The intersection precision $\geq 0.952$ lower
bound on all three corpora suggests $\sigma$ is
signal-orthogonal to softmax across architectures, not just
within a single backbone.

\noindent\textbf{Operational recipe.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Train or pick a KIE model; produce $\pi$ and softmax
$s$ per receipt.
\item Extract $M$ from the document (labeled fields if
available; OCR + multi-candidate $\tau$ otherwise).
\item Run $\sigma$ at $\varepsilon{=}0.02$. Use
$k_{\min}{=}2$ on labeled corpora; $k_{\min}{=}1$ on OCR
corpora.
\item Accept iff $\sigma{=}1$ and $s{\geq}$ a fixed-coverage
softmax threshold (intersection gate). Defer otherwise.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\noindent\textbf{(i)} $\sigma$ requires arithmetic closure of
the target field; it applies to totals, subtotals, taxes ---
not to non-numeric fields (vendor, date, address).
\noindent\textbf{(ii)} We evaluate one field (total) across
two backbones and three corpora. Multi-field $\sigma$
(simultaneous total/subtotal/tax verification with
cross-field constraints) is journal-version work.
\noindent\textbf{(iii)} The cardinality-guard inversion is
directly measured only on SROIE; the CORD-side claim is
structural reasoning, not measurement. WildReceipt uses the
default $k_{\min}{=}2$.
\noindent\textbf{(iv)} The tolerance $\varepsilon$ is fixed
at $0.02$ on integer-cent inputs; on jurisdictions with
fractional-cent rounding the $\varepsilon$ sweep may not stay
flat.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A generator-orthogonal precision gate based on subset-sum
verification yields, on a pooled three-corpus benchmark of
$n{=}919$ receipts spanning two backbones and three regimes:
\textbf{(F1)} Wilson 95\% precision lower bound $0.956$ on
the pooled intersection $\sigma\sqcap\text{softmax}$
($160/162$); the intersection lower bound exceeds both
single-gate lower bounds ($0.908$ for $\sigma$, $0.919$ for
softmax matched). Per-corpus lower bounds ($0.847$ on CORD,
$0.796$ on SROIE, $0.952$ on WildReceipt) span all three
regimes;
\textbf{(F2)} pooled McNemar non-significance
($\chi^2{=}0.012$, $p{=}0.912$, $b{=}163, c{=}166$), with
WildReceipt $b{=}c{=}91$ exactly --- the orthogonality the
intersection exploits;
\textbf{(F3)} a regime-mechanistic explanation including the
encoder-only WildReceipt case where $\sigma$ and softmax are
equally precise alone but their intersection rises to
$113/114$. The verifier adds $0.0016\%$ to end-to-end latency
and is precision-stable under up to $40\%$ money-line noise.
